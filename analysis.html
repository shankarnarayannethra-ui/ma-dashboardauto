ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK

Six questions behind every deal.

Use this structure to turn a headline into a balanced, decision-useful assessment.

01

Long-term goals

How does the transaction support growth, capabilities, customers, market share, or diversification?

02

Cultural differences

Compare leadership style, incentives, decision speed, risk tolerance, and employee expectations.

03

Geographic distance

Consider time zones, language, regulation, political risk, travel, and operating complexity.

04

Financial valuation

Review price, premium, financing, comparable deals, synergies, and debt burden.

05

Shareholder value

Separate the immediate market reaction from the long-term earnings and cash-flow case.

06

Summary of intent

Explain plainly what management says it wants and what may actually motivate the deal.
